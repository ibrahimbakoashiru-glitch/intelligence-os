
A human-centered operating system designed to disappear into the background,
so people can focus on life, creativity, and meaning — not technology.

## Why Intelligence OS?
Modern operating systems demand attention.
They interrupt, distract, and overwhelm users.

Intelligence OS does the opposite.

It learns quietly.
It adapts gently.
It works calmly.

The goal is not power.
The goal is peace.

## Core Philosophy
- The OS should understand the user, not the other way around
- Technology should reduce stress, not create it
- Intelligence should be embedded, not exposed
- Control belongs to the user, always

## What Makes It Different
- No constant notifications
- No forced workflows
- No aggressive updates
- No ecosystem lock-in

Just a system that feels right.

## Status
This project is in its early design phase.
We are defining principles, architecture, and long-term vision
before writing production code.

## Vision
Intelligence OS is not built for today’s devices only.
It is designed for future hardware, new interaction models,
and a world where technology respects human attention.
