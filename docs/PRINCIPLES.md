# Principles of Intelligence OS

Intelligence OS (INT-OS) is guided by unbreakable principles to ensure it serves humans, respects freedom, and evolves continuously.

## 1. Human-Centered
- The OS understands the user, not the other way around.
- Decisions prioritize user comfort, clarity, and wellbeing.

## 2. Calm Technology
- No unnecessary notifications.
- No forced workflows.
- Minimal cognitive load.

## 3. Privacy First
- User data belongs to the user.
- No tracking or hidden collection.
- AI works locally whenever possible.

## 4. Modular & Adaptive
- Components evolve independently.
- Hardware agnostic: works on new devices seamlessly.
- AI intelligence adapts to individual users over time.

## 5. Open & Collaborative
- Fully open-source.
- Encourages community contributions.
- Transparent processes: everyone can see how the system evolves.

## 6. Continuous Evolution
- No version stagnation.
- Updates are intelligent and seamless.
- System grows with users and new technology.

## 7. Ethical AI
- AI decisions always respect human autonomy.
- No manipulation, no coercion.
- Transparency in AI behavior.

---

INT-OS is not just software. It is a philosophy that guides how technology should serve humanity.
