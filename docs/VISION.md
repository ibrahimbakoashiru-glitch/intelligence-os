# Vision of Intelligence OS

Intelligence OS (INT-OS) is a human-centered operating system designed to fade into the background,
letting users focus on creativity, life, and meaning — not technology.

## Our Goal
To create an OS that:
- Learns and adapts to the user naturally
- Reduces stress and cognitive load
- Optimizes hardware efficiently
- Respects user privacy and autonomy

## Why it Matters
Modern operating systems distract, overwhelm, and lock users into ecosystems.
INT-OS removes barriers, giving everyone control and freedom, while providing a calm,
delightful, and intelligent experience.

## Future-Proof
INT-OS is designed not only for today’s devices but for future hardware and interaction models.
It evolves continuously, with modular intelligence components that adapt to changing needs.

## Open & Collaborative
- Fully open-source
- Modular design for easy integration
- Community-driven evolution

INT-OS is not just software; it is a philosophy, a movement, and a tool
that makes technology truly serve humans.
